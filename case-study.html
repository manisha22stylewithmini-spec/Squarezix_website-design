FAQs

Got questions about our work?

The questions people ask most after reading our case studies, answered by the team that did the work.

What does a Squarezix case study include?

Each one covers the problem the client came to us with, what we changed, the services involved and the numbers that moved — so you can judge the work by results, not just visuals.

How long does a project like these take?

It depends on scope. A focused website rebuild like Digital Stream went from brief to launch in nine weeks; larger builds, rebrands and ongoing SEO run longer. You’ll get a custom proposal with a timeline within five days of our first call.

Do you only work with businesses in Dubai?

No. We’re based in Business Bay, Dubai, and work with brands across the UAE, the wider GCC and Asia.

Which services do these projects cover?

Branding, web design, web development, e-commerce, SEO and AI search visibility, social media, content, paid marketing and website maintenance — often several on the same project.

Can you take on a project like one of these?

Very likely. Book a free 30-minute strategy call, tell us what you’re trying to change, and we’ll show you the closest work we’ve done and how we’d approach yours — no obligation, no hard sell.
