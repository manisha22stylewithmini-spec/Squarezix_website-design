FAQs

Questions about Concrete CMS Development?

Clear answers to the questions businesses ask us most about Concrete CMS Development.

What is Concrete CMS?

An open-source content management system known for in-context editing, strong permissions and security.

Is Concrete CMS good for large organisations?

Yes. Its permissions, workflows and security suit government, education and enterprise websites.

Can you upgrade our old Concrete CMS site?

Yes. We upgrade older versions and fix compatibility issues along the way.

Can you migrate to Concrete CMS from another platform?

Yes. We migrate content and set up redirects to protect your rankings.
