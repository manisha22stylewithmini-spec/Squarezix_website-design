FAQs

Have questions about getting your ecommerce website to rank higher?

Platforms, timelines, costs and payments — everything businesses ask us before building an ecommerce website in Dubai.

How do I get started with your ecommerce website development services?

Getting started is simple. Contact our team with your requirements, and we’ll schedule a consultation to understand your business, recommend the right approach, and provide a tailored proposal. From there, we handle everything from planning to launch.

Can you help with ecommerce marketing after development?

Yes, beyond ecommerce website development, we support businesses with SEO, PPC, and conversion optimization strategies. Our goal is not just to build your store but to help you generate consistent traffic, leads, and sales.

Do you integrate payment gateways and shipping solutions?

Yes, we integrate secure payment gateways and shipping solutions tailored for the UAE market. This includes local and international payment options, automated shipping calculations, and real-time tracking to enhance the customer experience.

Do you offer custom ecommerce website development solutions?

Yes, we provide fully custom ecommerce website development for businesses with unique requirements. Using frameworks like Laravel and headless commerce technologies, we build tailored platforms that align perfectly with your workflows, integrations, and scalability needs.

Can you redesign or upgrade my existing ecommerce website?

Absolutely. We specialize in redesigning outdated ecommerce websites and upgrading them to modern, high-performing platforms. Whether you need better UI/UX, improved speed, or migration to a new platform, we ensure a seamless transition without losing your existing data or SEO value.

Do you provide SEO-friendly ecommerce website development?

Yes, all our ecommerce websites are built with Ecommerce SEO best practices from the ground up. We optimize site structure, URLs, page speed, mobile responsiveness, and schema markup to help your store rank higher on search engines and AI-driven search platforms, driving organic traffic and long-term growth.

How long does it take to develop an ecommerce website?

The timeline for ecommerce website development typically ranges from 2 to 8 weeks, depending on the scope and features. Simple stores can be launched quickly, while custom-built platforms or large product catalogs may take longer. We follow a structured process to ensure timely delivery without compromising quality.

Which platform is best for ecommerce website development in Dubai?

The best platform depends on your business size, product range, and growth plans. Shopify is ideal for quick deployment and ease of use, Magento suits large-scale and complex ecommerce operations, WooCommerce is great for flexibility, and Laravel or headless solutions are perfect for fully customized platforms. Our team helps you choose the right platform based on your specific needs.

How much does ecommerce website development cost in Dubai?

The cost of ecommerce website development in Dubai depends on the complexity, platform, features, and customization required. Basic ecommerce websites may start from a few thousand dirhams, while advanced, enterprise-level platforms can cost significantly more. At SquareZix, we provide customized pricing based on your requirements to ensure you get maximum ROI without unnecessary expenses.

What is included in your ecommerce website development services in Dubai?

Our ecommerce website development services in Dubai cover everything required to launch and scale a successful online store. This includes business analysis, platform selection (Shopify, Magento, WooCommerce, or custom), UI/UX design, secure payment gateway integration, product catalog setup, mobile optimization, SEO-ready structure, and post-launch support. We deliver a complete, ready-to-sell solution tailored to your business goals.
