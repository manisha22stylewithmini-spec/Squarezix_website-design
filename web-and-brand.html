How we work

From brief to launch, like clockwork

  1. 01Week 1

    Discover

    We learn the business before we touch a pixel, so every later decision has a reason behind it.

    What happens

    • Stakeholder workshops
    • Audience and competitor research
    • Site, SEO and analytics audit

    You get

    • Discovery report
    • KPI map
    • Project roadmap
  2. 02Week 2

    Position

    We define who you are, who you serve and why you win, then put it into words.

    What happens

    • Positioning framework
    • Naming and messaging
    • Tone of voice

    You get

    • Brand strategy deck
    • Messaging framework
  3. 03Weeks 3–5

    Design

    Identity first, then the experience: every screen designed and signed off before build.

    What happens

    • Visual identity system
    • UX wireframes
    • High-fidelity UI and motion prototype

    You get

    • Brand guidelines
    • UI kit
    • Clickable prototype
  4. 04Weeks 6–8

    Build

    Engineered for speed, SEO and content-editor happiness on the platform that fits you.

    What happens

    • Front-end and CMS build
    • Performance and SEO setup
    • QA across devices

    You get

    • Staging site
    • Editor training
    • Launch checklist
  5. 05Week 9

    Launch

    A staged rollout and a brand reveal that makes the market take notice from day one.

    What happens

    • Staged rollout and redirects
    • Tracking and analytics
    • Brand reveal campaign

    You get

    • Analytics dashboard
    • Launch assets
  6. 06Ongoing

    Launch is day one. Then we measure, test and improve in quarterly growth sprints.

    What happens
