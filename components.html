Component library

38 reusable components and the design tokens behind them, rendered by the same builder functions the real pages use. This is not a website page: pick components here and combine them with plain data to build any page. Foundations mirror the Figma file Squarezix — Design System; components are documented from the production code, which is the source of the final design.

How to use: each card shows the live component, its variants and states, its props, and a one-line call. Builders live in scripts/build_service_pages.py and scripts/build_company_pages.py; styles in service-static.css, company.css, site-ui.css; tokens in tokens.css.

                  Strategic Website Migration

                  Website migration is much more than copying files from one server to another. It is a technical process that directly affects your website’s visibility, customer experience, search rankings, and revenue. A poorly executed migration can take months to recover from. That’s why businesses trust experienced migration specialists to manage every technical aspect before, during, and after launch. At SquareZix, we follow a structured migration methodology that minimizes risk while protecting your website’s long-term performance. Every migration includes detailed planning, URL redirect mapping, database validation, technical SEO implementation, and continuous monitoring to ensure a successful transition. Whether your website serves local customers in Dubai or international audiences, our migration strategy is designed to safeguard your online visibility and business continuity.
