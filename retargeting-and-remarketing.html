FAQs

Questions about Retargeting & Remarketing?

Clear answers to the questions businesses ask us most about Retargeting & Remarketing.

What is the difference between retargeting and remarketing?

The terms are often used interchangeably. Both show ads to people who already visited your website or engaged with your brand.

Is retargeting expensive?

Usually not. Audiences are smaller and warmer, so retargeting often delivers some of the lowest costs per conversion.

Will people get annoyed by repeated ads?

We set frequency caps and refresh creatives so ads stay helpful rather than repetitive.

Do I need a pixel or tag on my website?

Yes. We set up the Google tag and platform pixels so we can build your audiences.
