Questions about Social & App Advertising?

Clear answers to the questions businesses ask us most about Social & App Advertising.

Which platforms do you run app install ads on?

Google’s app campaigns, which reach Google Play and Search, plus social platforms such as Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.

How do you make sure installs turn into active users?

We optimise campaigns for in-app actions, not just installs, so you reach users likely to engage.

Do you create the ad creatives?
