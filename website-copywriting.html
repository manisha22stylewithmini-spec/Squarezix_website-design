Questions about Website Copywriting?

Clear answers to the questions businesses ask us most about Website Copywriting.

Do you write in Arabic as well as English?

Yes. We write in both languages and adapt copy for each audience rather than translating word for word.

Will the copy be SEO-friendly?

Yes. We research keywords and search intent first, then write copy that reads naturally and ranks.

How many revisions are included?

Each page includes revision rounds so the final copy matches your voice and goals.

Yes. We audit your current pages and rewrite those that underperform.
